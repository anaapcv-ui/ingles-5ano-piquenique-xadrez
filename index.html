🚨 Pegadinhas para não cair na prova

  1. There aren't e There isn't têm apóstrofo ('). ❌ There arent   ✅ There aren't
  2. Não junte There's com isn't. ❌ Theres isn't any cheese   ✅ There isn't any cheese
  3. Plural pede There are. ❌ There's some mushrooms   ✅ There are some mushrooms
  4. too tem dois o. ❌ There are to many sausages   ✅ There are too many sausages
  5. enough vem antes da comida. ❌ There's honey enough   ✅ There's enough honey
  6. ❌ There AreA'nt trees   ✅ There aren't enough trees
  7. Escreva certinho: cheese (dois e), salad, mushrooms (com s), shish kebabs.
  8. carton se escreve c-a-r-t-o-n. ❌ a canton of juice   ✅ a carton of juice
  9. O mel vai no pote: ✅ a jar of honey (no Workbook, "a carton of honey" era uma etiqueta trocada!)
  10. Em Two trains traveled toward the tree, só trains, traveled e tree têm o som tr. Two e toward não!
